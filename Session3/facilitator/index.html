SESSION 3: EMPLOYEE VS BOSS

Goal: Understanding TFN vs ABN & The 30% Tax Rule.

Part 1: The Hook (Jack vs Jill)

Jack (Employee): Paid $1,000/wk net. Can spend it all.
Jill (Contractor): Paid $1,400/wk. Thinks she is rich. Spends it all.

Result: Jill has a $10,000 tax bill at end of year and no money to pay it.

EMPLOYEE (TFN)
  • Pros: Sick leave, Annual Leave, Super paid for you. Tax deducted automatically.
  • Cons: Fixed wage. Harder to claim some deductions. Building someone else's dream.
CONTRACTOR (ABN)
  • Pros: Unlimited earning potential. Tax deductions. Freedom.
  • Cons: No sick pay. Must save own Tax (BAS). Must pay own Super.

GAME: "WHO PAYS?"

Scenario Answer Why?
1. Steel Cap Boots BOTH Occupational PPE is deductible for everyone.
2. Public Liability Insurance CONTRACTOR Employee is covered by boss. Contractor needs own.
3. Drilled through a water pipe CONTRACTOR If you are ABN, you fix it. If TFN, Boss fixes it.
4. Speeding fine in work Ute NEITHER ATO never pays fines. You pay.
THE 30% RULE

"If you switch to ABN: Transfer 30% of every single payment into a separate 'Tax Account'. Do not touch it."
