SESSION 9: MOVING OUT

Goal: Rental Markets & The "Startup Cost" of Adulthood.

Part 1: The Hook ("The $4,000 Doorway")

"Walking through your own front door costs $4,000 before you even sleep there."

  • Bond (4 weeks): ~$2,000
  • Rent Advance (4 weeks): ~$2,000
  • Connection Fees & Moving Van: ~$500

Part 2: The Rental Competition

Real Estate agents judge you on 3 things:

  1. Ability to Pay: Payslips (Session 4).
  2. History: Credit Score (Session 6).
  3. Character: References.

The 30% Rule: Agents rarely approve you if rent is >30% of your income.

GAME: "THE REALITY RECEIPT"

Guess the monthly cost (Price is Right style):

  • Electricity (2 people) $150 - $200 / month
  • Internet (NBN) ~$80 / month
  • Block of Cheese (1kg) ~$14.00 (Shocking!)
  • Groceries (1 person) ~$600 / month
STRATEGIC PATIENCE

"Every year you live at home with parents is a $15,000 head start on your house deposit. Don't rush out just to look cool."
