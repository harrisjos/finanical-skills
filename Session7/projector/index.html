Session 7

GOOD VS BAD DEBT

Depreciation & Tax Deductions

THE MOST EXPENSIVE SMELL

Who likes "New Car Smell"?

It costs $10,000.

The second you drive a new car off the lot, it loses ~20% of its value. That is depreciation.

THE ATO TEST

BAD DEBT (Toy)

Goes DOWN in value.

  • Personal Cars
  • Holidays on credit
  • Clothes / BNPL
  • Interest is NOT tax deductible.

GOOD DEBT (Tool)

Goes UP or Earns INCOME.

  • Work Vehicle (Ute)
  • Tools / Equipment
  • A House (usually)
  • Interest IS tax deductible.

THE $50K SHOWDOWN

Both borrow $50k @ 8% over 5 years.

Feature Sports Sedan (Personal) Work Ute (Business)
Value after 5 yrs ~$20,000 ~$25,000
Total Repaid ~$60,000 ~$60,000
Tax Deduction? NO ($0) YES (Interest & Dep)
Result Paid $60k for a $20k car. The tool made you money.

THE 1/10th RULE

If buying a personal car (not a tax deduction):

Repayments should never exceed 10% of your take-home pay.

Earn $800/wk? Max car payment is $80/wk. Don't let the car own you.
