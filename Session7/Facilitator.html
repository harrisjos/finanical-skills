SESSION 7: GOOD VS BAD DEBT

Goal: Depreciation, Tax Deductions & Vehicle Finance.

Part 1: The Hook ("New Car Smell")

"New car smell costs $10,000. That's the sound of 20% depreciation the second you leave the lot."

WHITEBOARD: THE $50k SHOWDOWN
BAD DEBT (The Toy)

Financed Sports Sedan (Personal Use)

  • Loan: $50k @ 8%
  • Depreciation: Car worth $20k in 5 yrs.
  • Tax Deduction: $0.
  • Result: Paid $60k to own a $20k asset.
GOOD DEBT (The Tool)

Work Ute (100% Business Use)

  • Loan: $50k @ 8%
  • Income: Allows you to earn $80k/yr.
  • Tax Deduction: Interest & Depreciation are deductible.
  • Result: Gov helps pay for it. Asset makes money.

Part 3: The Rules

  • Bad Debt: Borrowing for things that go DOWN in value (Cars, Holidays, Clothes). Interest is NOT deductible.
  • Good Debt: Borrowing for things that go UP in value or earn INCOME (Houses, Tools, Education).
THE 1/10th RULE

"If buying a personal car: Repayments should never be more than 10% of your take-home pay. Don't let the car own you."
