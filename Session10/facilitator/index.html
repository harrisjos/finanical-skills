SESSION 10: THE SIDE HUSTLE

Goal: From Apprentice to CEO (Pricing & Brand).

Part 1: The Hook ("Saturday Morning Cash")

"Your mates work at Maccas for $22/hr. You have a skill worth $60/hr."

But... if you don't treat it like a business, you will lose money and get sued.

Part 2: The Quote Formula

Materials (+Markup) + Labor + Overheads = PRICE

  • Markup (10-15%): Pays for your time driving to Bunnings to get the wood.
  • Labor: Be realistic. If it takes 4 hours, quote 5.
  • Mate's Rates: "If it takes more than 1 hour, it's a job. Friends pay full price to support your business."
