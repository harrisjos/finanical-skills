SESSION 6: ADULT REPORT CARD

Goal: Credit Scores & How to protect your file.

Part 1: The Hook ("Computer Says No")

"A $200 unpaid phone bill at age 18 can stop you buying a house at age 25."

Welcome to the Adult Report Card. Unlike school, your grades stick for 5-7 years.

Part 2: The Score (0 - 1200)

  • Green Zone (800+): Banks fight for you. Low interest rates.
  • Red Zone (<500):< /strong> Instant decline or "Dodgy Car Yard" interest rates.

What kills it? Defaults (60 days late), Missed Payments (14 days late), and Shopping Around (Too many enquiries).

GAME: "UP, DOWN, OR BOMB?"
1. You pay off car loan perfectly. UP
2. You apply for 4 credit cards in one week. DOWN (Desperate behaviour)
3. You ignore a Telstra bill because you moved house. BOMB (Default - 5 Years)
4. You check your own score on an app. NO CHANGE (Soft check)
