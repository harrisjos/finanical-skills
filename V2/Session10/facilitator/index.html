Jim's Dog Wash. Jim's Antennas. Jim's Fencing. Jim's Cleaning. It all started with a mower and a letterbox drop.

Part 2: The Quote Formula

Materials (+Markup) + Labor + Overheads = PRICE

  • Markup (10-15%): Pays for your time driving to Bunnings to get the wood.
  • Labor: Be realistic. If it takes 4 hours, quote 5.
  • Mate's Rates: "If it takes more than 1 hour, it's a job. Friends pay full price to support your business."

The Hidden Cost of "Cashies"

Cash jobs feel like free money. But if you want to build a real business — they're actually the most expensive money you earn.

Risk 1: The ATO

You never know who works for the ATO. It could be the client. The neighbour. The person you're bragging to at the pub.

Back-taxes + interest + penalties = years of savings wiped.

Risk 2: Ghost Money

Banks lend on income they can see. Earn $50k declared + $50k cash? The bank thinks you earn $50k.

When to Get Help: You Don't Need to Know Everything

"You need to know who to call — not how to do their job."

Accountant
  • Setting up your ABN or Pty Ltd
  • What you can and can't claim
  • BAS lodgements (GST)
  • Tax planning as you grow

When: Before you register anything. Most offer a free first consult.

Fair Work / IR Advisor
  • When you hire your first employee
  • Award rates & pay obligations
  • Casual vs permanent rules
  • Superannuation requirements

When: Before you take on any staff.

Insurance Broker
  • Public Liability (non-negotiable)
  • Tools & equipment cover
  • Professional indemnity
  • Income protection

When: Day one. Before your first job.
