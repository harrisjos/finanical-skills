PRICING FOR PROFIT

Don't guess. Use the formula.

Materials (+Markup) + Labor = PRICE
  • Markup (15%): Pays for your time driving to Bunnings.
  • Labor Rate: This must cover your invisible costs (Fuel, Tools, Tax).
  • The Rule: "If I quote $60/hr, I keep $40. The rest is for the business."

ACTIVITY: QUOTE THE JOB

Scenario: Build a timber gate. Materials cost $150. Time: 4 hours.

The Amateur Quote

$150 (Mats) + $100 (Labor guess)

= $250.00

You forgot fuel, screws, and driving time. You actually worked for $15/hr.

VS

The Pro Quote

$172.50 (Mats + 15%)
+ $240.00 (4hrs @ $60)

= $412.50

You look professional. You cover your costs. You make profit.
