Session 10: The Cash Jobs Question
THE SCENARIO

Jake is a 3rd-year apprentice electrician. On weekends he does side jobs — resi rewires, fan installs, power points. He earns around $800/week cash on top of his apprenticeship wage of $700/week.

Jake doesn't declare the cash. He figures it's easier that way.

1. Jake wants to buy a house in 3 years. His total real income is $1,500/wk ($78,000/yr). But the bank can only see $700/wk ($36,400/yr). What problem does this create?

2. Jake also wants to buy a $15,000 van to grow his business. What will the bank's decision likely be based on his declared income? What could he do differently?

3. The ATO sends Jake a letter. They've noticed his bank deposits are much higher than his declared income. He owes $24,000 in back-taxes, interest, and penalties. Where did this figure likely come from?
