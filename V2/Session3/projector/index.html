THE $200,000 MISTAKE

A plumber switched to ABN and invoiced $1,800 a week. He felt rich — new ute, holidays, tools on credit. He never saved for tax.

Four years later, the ATO caught up.

$200,000 tax debt — plus interest and penalties.
Lost his ute. Lost his tools. Lost his house deposit.
All because he didn't know the rules.

What could he have done differently from day one?

THE GOLDEN RULE FOR ABNs

THE 30% RULE

Every time you get paid, transfer 30% into a separate savings account immediately.

Do not touch it. That money belongs to the ATO.

SOLE TRADER VS COMPANY

SOLE TRADER (ABN)

  • Cheapest to set up
  • You = the business (legally)
  • You keep all the profit
  • Personally liable for debts
  • Tax at your personal rate

Best for: Side hustles & getting started.

COMPANY (Pty Ltd)

  • Separate legal entity
  • Your personal assets are protected
  • Flat tax rate: 25–30%
  • More admin & annual costs
  • Looks professional to banks

Best for: Scaling up & protecting assets.

Don't register anything until you've spoken to an accountant.

THE HIDDEN COST OF "CASHIES"

The ATO Risk

You never know who works for the ATO. It could be the neighbour you build a fence for.

Back taxes + interest + penalties = years of savings gone.

The Ghost Money Problem

Banks lend on income they can see.

Earn $50k declared + $50k cash?

The bank thinks you earn $50k.

Home loan: DECLINED.
Business loan: DECLINED.

"Declare your income — it's how you prove you deserve the loan."
