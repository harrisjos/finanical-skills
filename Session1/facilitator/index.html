SESSION 1: THE WEALTH MINDSET

Goal: Redefine wealth as Net Worth, not Income.

Part 1: The Hook (5 Mins)

"The Tale of Two Apprentices"

Script: "Picture two apprentices, both 19, earning $800/week.

Apprentice A (Flashy Phil): Drives a financed $70k Ranger Raptor. Newest iPhone. Buys lunch daily. Looks rich. Net Worth: -$50,000 (Debt).
Apprentice B (Smart Sam): Drives a paid-off Hilux. Brings lunch. Owns his tools. Puts $100/wk into shares. Looks average. Net Worth: +$30,000.

The Lesson: Phil is owned by the bank. Sam owns his future. Today we learn the difference."

Part 2: Core Definitions

Term Definition Tradie Example
ASSET Something that puts money IN your pocket. Your Tools (earn income), Shares (dividends), Investment Property (rent).
LIABILITY Something that takes money OUT of your pocket. Car Loan (interest + depreciation), Jet Ski, Afterpay debt.

INTERACTIVE GAME: "TOOLBOX OR TRASH?"

Facilitator reads item -> Class shouts "ASSET" (Toolbox) or "LIABILITY" (Trash).

  • 1. A Jet Ski: Liability (Fuel, Rego, no income)
  • 2. Milwaukee Impact Driver: Asset (Makes you work faster = money)
  • 3. $5,000 Gaming PC: Liability (Electricity cost, unless you stream)
  • 4. Investment Property: Asset (Tenant pays mortgage)
  • 5. Uber Eats App: Liability (Pure cash drain)
  • 6. An ABN: Asset (Allows tax deductions)
HOMEWORK CHALLENGE

"The Rule of Thumb: For every 'Toy' (Liability) you buy, you must put an equal amount of cash into an 'Asset' (Savings/Shares)."
