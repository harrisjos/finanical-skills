SESSION 4: PAYCHECK REALITY

Goal: Reading complex payslips (Allowances, Super, TAFE).

Part 1: The Hook ("The Missing $300")

"You calculated $1,100 in your head. You received $800 in the bank. Why?"

If you can't read your payslip, you might be missing hundreds in unpaid allowances.

Part 2: Anatomy of a Victorian Tradie Payslip

  • 1. Allowances: Travel, Tools, Site Allowance. (The hidden money).
  • 2. Deductions: PAYG Tax (Roads/Hospitals), Union Fees.
  • 3. Superannuation: 12% paid ON TOP. Not deducted.
  • 4. TAFE Fees: Usually Paid by Boss! (Unlike Uni HECS debt).
ACTIVITY: THE PAYSLIP DETECTIVE

Distribute the Handout.

Scenario: Tom worked Mon-Fri + 4hrs Sat. Drove own car 5 days.

The 3 Mistakes to find:
  1. Saturday Rate listed as Ordinary ($22). Should be 1.5x.
  2. Travel Allowance listed as 3 units. Should be 5 units.
  3. Super listed as 10%. Should be 12%.
THE 3-SECOND CHECK

"Every Thursday, check: 1. Hours (Overtime?) 2. Allowances (Travel?) 3. Super ($ amount?)"
