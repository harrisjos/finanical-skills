THE CHAINSAW

Credit Cards & BNPL: Tool or Trap?

THE BANK'S FAVOURITE CUSTOMER

Customer A ("The Deadbeat")

Pays card in full every month. Gets free flights. Pays $0 interest.

Bank hates them.

Customer B ("The Cash Cow")

Makes the Minimum Repayment every month.

Bank loves them.

THE TOOL (Credit Card)

  • Pros: Up to 55 days interest-free. Use bank's money for cash flow. Rewards.
  • The Trap: 20% Interest if you miss the date by one day.

THE LEECH (BNPL)

  • The Trap: "Death by a thousand cuts."
  • Micro-payments ($20 here, $50 there) strangle your weekly cash flow.
  • Banks hate seeing it on loan apps.

THE $5,000 MISTAKE

You bought a Gaming PC/Bike on a credit card (18%).
You only make the Minimum Repayment.

33
YEARS to pay off
$12k

By the time you pay it off, you'll be 50 and the PC will be junk.

THE GOLDEN RULE

If you can't pay cash for it right now,

Don't put it on credit.

Set up "Auto-Pay Full Balance" on credit cards, or cut them up.
