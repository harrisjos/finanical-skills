SESSION 2: THE 8TH WONDER

Goal: Understand Compound Interest & The Cost of "Small" Spending.

Part 1: The Hook (The Servo Tax)

"The Daily Smoko Ritual"

  • 1 x V Energy / Iced Coffee: $4.50
  • 1 x Pie / Sausage Roll: $5.50
  • Total Daily Cost: $10.00

Over a 45-year career = $112,500 cash.

"If invested at 8%, that pie cost you $1.5 Million in retirement money."

Part 2: The Snowball Effect

Compound Interest is interest earning interest. Einstein called it the 8th Wonder of the World.

Year 1: You save $1,000. Bank pays $50 interest.
Year 2: You earn interest on $1,050. (The snowball grows).
INTERACTIVE: "THE TIME MACHINE"
Tradie A: "Early Eric"
  • Starts investing at Age 18.
  • Invests $5,000/year.
  • STOPS at Age 28. (Invested $50k total).
  • Waits until Age 60.

Balance: $787,000

Tradie B: "Late Luke"
  • Parties until 28. Starts at Age 28.
  • Invests $5,000/year.
  • Continues until Age 60. (Invested $160k total).

Balance: $730,000

Eric worked less but earned more because he started early.

THE TAKEAWAY

"You have the winning lottery ticket: TIME. Don't waste it. Even $20 a week counts if you start at 18."
